PolyBridge

Connections, in your own terms.

PolyBridge is a new project for folks in poly relationships, offering easy-to-use graphical tools to describe and manage those relationships. You can build a picture of your relationship network and choose what information to share, with no technical background needed.

You decide how to describe your relationships and what your network looks like, with the freedom to reflect the connections in your life as they grow and change.

Our goals

Represent relationships as people live them

We’re building PolyBridge to support relationship networks of different shapes and sizes. Anyone should be able to describe their relationships in their own terms, free from prescribed hierarchies or academic models.

Give people control over their information

You should control how your information is organized, how it’s presented, and who you share it with. Above all, you should retain ownership of your data.

Respect individual identities

PolyBridge makes room for the names, pronouns, relationship descriptions, and identities people use for themselves. Each person should retain control over their own unique identity when participating in a shared relationship space.

Support plurals

We’re planning for plurals and their relationships from the beginning, with thoughtful choices about how identities are represented and how information is shared.

Make it part of your daily routine

We’re planning tools for the everyday details of poly relationships, giving each partner ways to share who they’re currently dating, coordinate whose night it is to spend time together or take care of something, and describe kink interests or activities they choose to share.

These features are part of our design goals, with each person controlling what they record and share.

PolyBridge is an independent project by Needs of the Many.
