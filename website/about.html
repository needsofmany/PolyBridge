About the project

Relationships, on your own terms.

PolyBridge is an independent, open-source project by Needs of the Many. We believe poly relationships deserve practical tools that respect the people in them and the ways they choose to live.

We’re building a place where people can describe their connections in their own language, coordinate life together, and retain ownership of their information.
