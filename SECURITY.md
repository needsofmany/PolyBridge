# Security policy

PolyBridge is intended to handle relationship information that people choose to record and share. Treat credentials, authentication material, permission settings, and private relationship data as sensitive.

Do not commit or publish:

- passwords, tokens, keys, or session credentials
- authentication or permission details that could grant access
- real relationship records, private identities, or personal communications
- information disclosed by accident in logs, screenshots, or examples

## Reporting

Send an initial security report privately to info@thepolybridge.org.

Start with a general description of the problem. Do not include credentials or real personal records in the first message. The maintainers can ask for the least information needed to understand the report.

This policy does not promise a response time.
